Who does what

Roles & Responsibilities

Clear roles prevent confusion during an incident. Here is who is involved and when.

Incident Response Team / IT Security Team

Main duties

  • Investigate and analyse reported incidents
  • Contain and eradicate threats
  • Collect and preserve evidence
  • Lead technical recovery

Involved when

Every confirmed or suspected incident, from the first report onward.

Incident Manager

Main duties

  • Coordinate the whole response
  • Assign tasks and track progress
  • Decide on escalation and keep the incident log
  • Lead the post-incident review

Involved when

Medium severity and above, or whenever several teams must work together.

IT Help Desk

Main duties

  • Receive reports from students and staff
  • Log tickets and do first-level triage
  • Give immediate safety advice to the user
  • Pass incidents to the security team quickly

Involved when

At the very start: the first point of contact for most reports.

College Management

Main duties

  • Approve major decisions (e.g. shutting down systems)
  • Provide resources and authority
  • Handle serious impact on academics and reputation

Involved when

High or Critical incidents, or any decision with major business impact.

Data Protection / Legal Officer

Main duties

  • Assess whether personal data was exposed
  • Advise on legal and regulatory notification duties
  • Review communications and evidence handling

Involved when

Data breaches, lost devices with sensitive data, or any legal exposure.

Communication Team

Main duties

  • Prepare accurate notices for students, staff and parents
  • Handle media or public questions
  • Keep messages consistent and timely

Involved when

Incidents affecting many users, public services or the college's reputation.
