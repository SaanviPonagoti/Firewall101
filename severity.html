Severity & Escalation

Severity decides how fast the college must respond and who must be told. Response times below are sample targets.

LevelExample incidentsRequired response timeWho to notify
LowSuspicious email reported. No click, no data entered.Within 24 hoursIT Help Desk; IT Security Team informed
MediumOne student or staff account compromised.Within 4 hoursIT Security Team, Incident Manager, affected user's department
HighMalware on a lab or office computer.Within 1 hourIT Security Team, Incident Manager, department head, College Management
CriticalRansomware, major data breach, or college-wide service disruption.Immediate (within 15 minutes)Entire Incident Response Team, College Management, Data Protection/Legal Officer, Communication Team

Escalation rule of thumb

If the incident involves sensitive personal data, spreads to more than one system, or stops teaching or administration, treat it as at least one level higher and escalate.
